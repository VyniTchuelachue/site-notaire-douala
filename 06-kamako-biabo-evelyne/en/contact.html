Timing and tracking of your file

A notarial file goes through the office, then through several administrations. Here is what depends on each, so you always know where yours stands.

StageWho actsTiming
1Complete documentsYouThe clock starts once the file is complete.
2Checks and draft deedThe officeTiming given when the file is opened.
3SigningYou and the officeOn the date agreed together.
4RegistrationTax administrationAfter the duties are paid.
5Land registry or trade registerAdministrationsThe office follows up and keeps you informed.
