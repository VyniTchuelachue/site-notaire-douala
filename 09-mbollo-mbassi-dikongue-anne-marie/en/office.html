The notary

A notary is a public officer appointed by the State to receive the deeds that the parties must, or wish to, make authentic. The notary guarantees their date, keeps the original — the minute — and issues copies.

  • A deed that is proofIts content and date can only be challenged through a special procedure.
  • An enforceable deedWith the enforcement formula, it can be enforced without going to court.
  • A deed kept safeThe original stays at the office: you can obtain a copy years later.
