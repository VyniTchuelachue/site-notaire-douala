Frequently asked questions

Answers to the questions most often asked of a notary.

How can I avoid buying disputed land?

Prefer titled land and pay nothing until the notary has checked the title. The office requests a certificate of ownership from the land registry: it shows the current owner and any charges, such as a mortgage or an objection.

How much does a notarial deed cost?

Costs include duties and taxes paid to the State (registration, land registry) and the notary's fees, which are set by regulation. They depend on the type and value of the transaction: ask for an estimate before you start.

Can I buy land without travelling?

Yes. You give a power of attorney to a trusted person, who signs the deed of sale at the office in your name. The office then has the land title issued in your name.

Why sign a marriage contract?

It sets the regime that will apply to your assets during the marriage and in case of separation or death. It is signed before the notary, before the wedding.

How can I check that a notary is practising?

The National Chamber of Notaries of Cameroon publishes the list of practising notaries and their offices. If in doubt, ask the Chamber.

What is a land title?

It is the official document that establishes ownership of land in Cameroon. It is issued and kept by the land registry. Land without a title carries more risk: ask the notary for advice before buying.

Can the notary keep my will?

Yes. The will can be received by the notary as an authentic deed, or deposited with the notary. It is kept safe and found when needed.

How long does it take to set up a company?

It depends on how quickly the documents are gathered and on the registration formalities. The office tells you the expected timing when the file is opened.

Have a project? Let's talk.

Call or write to the office: you will know which documents to gather and what costs to expect.
