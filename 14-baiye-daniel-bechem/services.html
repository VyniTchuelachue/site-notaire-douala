Areas of practice

Land, estates, family, business: the deeds the office prepares, receives and keeps for you.

01

Land and buildings

Purchase, sale, exchange, long leases, mortgage loans. Before anything is signed, the office checks the land title and requests a certificate of ownership from the land registry.

Common deeds

  • Sale and purchase of titled land and buildings
  • Land title check and certificate of ownership before signing
  • Emphyteutic and long leases
  • Mortgage loans
  • Exchange, division and gifts of real estate
02

Estates

Inventory of assets, division between heirs, transfer of land titles and company shares to the heirs.

Common deeds

  • Inventory of the deceased's estate
  • Deed of division between heirs
  • Transfer of land titles to the heirs
  • Transfer of company shares
  • Support for heirs living abroad
03

Family

Marriage contracts, including for binational couples. Gifts to children or between spouses. Wills.

Common deeds

  • Marriage contract before the wedding
  • Change of matrimonial regime
  • Gifts to children or between spouses
  • Authentic will and deposit of a will
  • Binational couples: choice of applicable law
04

Business

Company formation under OHADA law (SARL, SAS, SA), capital increases, transfers of shares, minutes of general meetings.

Common deeds

  • Formation of SARL, SAS and SA under OHADA law
  • Drafting and amending articles of association
  • Capital increase or reduction
  • Transfer of shares
  • Minutes of general meetings, dissolution and liquidation
05

Powers of attorney

So that a trusted person can sign on your behalf in Cameroon: land purchase, estate, banking.

Common deeds

  • Power of attorney to buy or sell property
  • Power of attorney to settle an estate
  • Banking or administrative power of attorney
  • Power of attorney signed abroad and used in Cameroon
06

Authentication and copies

Filing of documents with the office's records, certified copies of deeds received by the office.

Common deeds

  • Filing of documents with the office's records
  • Certified copies of deeds
  • Certification of signatures and true copies
  • Safekeeping of original deeds by the office

You live outside Cameroon

Buying land back home, settling a parent's estate, setting up a company in Douala: most files move forward without you having to travel.

Before any payment, confirm the bank details by calling the office's landline.

  1. Write to the officeBy WhatsApp or email: the transaction, the property and your country of residence.
  2. Send your documentsFirst as scans for review; the originals follow by post or through your representative.
  3. Sign remotelyBy power of attorney: you sign the draft before a notary where you live, or have it legalised at the Cameroonian consulate.

How your file proceeds

  1. 1

    First contact

    By phone, WhatsApp or at the office: you describe the transaction you have in mind.

  2. 2

    Document list and estimate

    The office tells you which documents to provide and gives an estimate of the costs: registration duties, land registry fees, notary fees.

  3. 3

    Checks

    The office reviews the documents and runs the checks: land title, civil status, signatories' powers.

  4. 4

    Signing

    The deed is read and signed at the office, or by power of attorney if you cannot travel.

  5. 5

    Formalities

    Registration, then entry at the land registry or the trade register, as the case may be. You receive a certified copy of the deed.
